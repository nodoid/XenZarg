# Store listing: XenZarg

Everything to paste into App Store Connect (iOS and Mac), Google Play Console and Microsoft Partner Center. Character counts are within each store's limits (checked by `tools/check_listing.py`).

> **Before you submit:**
> - XenZarg reproduces the graphics, colours and play of the Oric games *Xenon 1* and *Zorgon's Revenge* (1983) and *Xenon 3 - The Genesis Probe* (IJK Software, 1985). Xenon 1 and Zorgon sprites, title artwork and scenery were captured from the originals; Xenon 3's graphics are reconstructed from its Oric screens and instructions. The stores reject apps that use other people's intellectual property without permission, and may ask you to prove you have the rights. Make sure you have permission from whoever now owns the rights to all three games.
> - The in-game credit reads "By PFJ, based on the IJK originals". The surviving tape inlays, reviews and the original code all credit the three games to **John S. Sinclair**, published by **IJK Software**. Check the attribution before you publish, and change `XenZargGame.BasedOn` if needed.

```
XENON 1
You are a Fleet Commander of the Xenon Space Academy. Fly to the planet Radon and protect it from the Zorgon Battle Star.
- Level 1: destroy the Bird-Aards wheeling in their hypnotic formation.
- Level 2: shoot the Large Aards. Each one clones when hit, and one clone dives at you and explodes. Keep away from the radiation!
- Level 3: steer through the meteorite storm.
- Level 4: land on planet Radon and fight off the parachuting Paratrons.
- Level 5: burn through the force field and hit the moving nuclear reactor to vaporise the Zorgon Battle Star. The faster you do it, the bigger the bonus.

ZORGON'S REVENGE

- Catch the swinging rod, ride the lift and dodge the spider.
- Head the Quadnogs' own shots back at them and get past the laser beams.
- Fly the Xenon ship against waves of aliens and their mothership.
- Ride the great bird up to the ledges.
Then lay the bridge, fire the castle cannons and topple Zorgon.

XENON 3 - THE GENESIS PROBE (1985)
Radon is dying. Fly the Genesis Probe to five neighbouring planets and teleport two of every species aboard before the radiation finishes the job.

- Terra: use the teleport beam for two of every species and catch the red barrels.
- Radon: dodge five waves of Zorgons. There is no weapon in the final storm.

AUTHENTIC

CONTROLS
Tilt to steer, and tap anywhere to fire or jump. Tap the fuel gauge to refuel. Tap with three fingers to pause.

FEATURES
- All three games in one continuous adventure, which gets harder each time round
- Nine skill levels
- Hall of Fame with name entry
- Instruction screens for every level
- No ads, no tracking and no internet connection needed

By PFJ, based on the IJK originals.
```

**Restricted capabilities** (Partner Center › Submission options › *Why does your app need these capabilities?*)

The package declares one restricted capability, `runFullTrust` (in `XenZarg.WindowsDX/Windows/AppxManifest.xml`). No other restricted or general capabilities are declared: not `internetClient`, file system access, webcam, microphone or location. Paste:
```
runFullTrust: XenZarg is a packaged desktop (Win32) game, built with .NET 10 and MonoGame and rendered with DirectX 11. Every packaged Win32 desktop app needs runFullTrust to start its executable (Windows.FullTrustApplication entry point). The game uses it only to run its own process. It does not use the internet, other apps or processes, the user's documents or system settings. It reads the keyboard, mouse and game controllers, draws with DirectX, plays sound with XAudio, and saves its high scores and settings in its own app data folder.
```

**Notes for certification**
```
No account, sign-in, network connection or purchase is needed; all content is available immediately. To play: press SPACE (or click) on the title, then SPACE on the menu to start. Z / X or the mouse move, SPACE or the left mouse button fires and jumps, cursor up or the right mouse button refuels, P pauses, Esc ends the game, F11 toggles full screen.
```
