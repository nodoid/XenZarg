# Privacy Policy: XenZarg

*Last updated: 5 October 2026*

XenZarg is a game written by Paul F. Johnson. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** XenZarg doesn't collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

## What stays on your device

The game saves two small files in its private storage on your device:

- **High scores:** the names you enter in the Hall of Fame, with your scores.
- **Settings:** your chosen skill level and sound volume.

These files never leave your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Motion sensors

On phones and tablets, the game reads your device's motion sensors only while you are playing, to steer. The readings are used immediately and are never stored or sent anywhere.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
